NEU Student

2D pixel platformer · Graduation thesis · Unity

A 2D pixel-art platformer I built solo in Unity as my IT graduation thesis at the National Economics University (NEU), Hanoi.

I drew every sprite by hand in Piskel, frame by frame: the hero, a NEU student in the university uniform, and a pillow demon enemy. Then I set up all animations in-engine.
